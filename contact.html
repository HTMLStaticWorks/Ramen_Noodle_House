Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful guidelines regarding walk-in policies, reservations, customs, and allergens.

We preserve 50% of our cedar counter and dining room for walk-in guests at all times to honor traditional neighborhood noodle shops. For parties of 2 to 6 seeking guaranteed seats, reservations can be placed online through our website. For parties of 7 or larger, please submit a group enquiry via the form above.

Kaedama is a cherished Fukuoka tradition: ordering an extra serving of hot, freshly boiled noodles to drop straight into your remaining broth. Be sure to leave at least half of your broth in your bowl, and simply ask your server for a "Kaedama" whenever you are ready!
