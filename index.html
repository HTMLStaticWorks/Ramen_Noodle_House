DINING CULTURE

The NAMI 4-Step Ramen Ritual

A truly great bowl of ramen is an active experience. Follow our master chef's ritual to unlock the fullness of flavor in every bite.

01

Inhale Aromatics

Before touching chopsticks, lean in and savor the fragrant tare, toasted sesame, and slow-rendered aromatics rising with the steam.

02

Sip The Pure Broth

Take three undisturbed spoonfuls of the broth alone. Experience the velvety mouthfeel and 18-hour collagen extraction before noodles interact.

03

Slurp With Gusto

Slurping cools the noodles and aerates the broth like fine wine, coating your palate and releasing maximum aromatic nuance.

04

Request Kaedama

Never let extraordinary broth go to waste. Signal your server for a fresh Kaedama (hot noodle refill) to drop straight into your remaining soup.
