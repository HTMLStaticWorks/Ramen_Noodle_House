How To Order Like A Tokyo Regular

At NAMI Noodle, every bowl can be tailored to match your exact taste preference. Let your server know your preferred parameters.

1. Noodle Firmness
  • Barikata: Extra firm, 30-sec boil
  • Katame (Recommended): Classic firm chew
  • Futsuu: Standard traditional softness
  • Yawa: Soft and supple
2. Broth Richness
  • Light (Kotteri Light): Leaner tare profile
  • Regular: Perfectly balanced collagen
  • Kotteri Heavy: Extra rendered pork fat emulsion for deep decadence
3. Roasted Garlic
  • None: Pure unadulterated broth
  • Standard: Subtle aromatic warmth
  • Roasted Mayu: Charred black garlic oil
  • Double Punch: Raw grated + fried chips
4. Spice Scale
  • Level 0: Gentle & comforting
  • Level 1 (Mild): Warm chili aroma
  • Level 2 (Medium): Tangy Sichuan burn
  • Level 3 (Demon): Ghost pepper & red oil

Dietary Notes & Kitchen Allergens

We take food safety seriously. Our plant-based broths are cooked in dedicated cookware. While we take every precaution against cross-contamination, our kitchen prepares items containing wheat, eggs, soy, pork, and sesame seeds. Please notify our service team of any severe allergies before ordering.
