PRECISION & METHOD

Made With Intention

Every step in our kitchen is calibrated to the second and degree. Consistency is how we achieve excellence day after day.

01

Prep & Sourcing

Berkshire pork bones blanched twice to purge impurities. 2-year aged kombu steeped in cold filtered spring water overnight.

02

18-Hour Boil

Vigorous thermal convection extracts collagen and marrow, transforming water and bone into a rich, silky emulsion.

03

45-Sec Pull

Noodles dropped in boiling kettles and strained with a crisp wrist snap to completely shed excess starch water.

04

Immediate Pour

Piped boiling broth over preheated ceramic bowls. Plated with torched chashu and served to your seat within 12 seconds.
